## Key features

- Edits DOCX, XLSX and PPTX directly; page view, ribbon, styles, track changes and comparing versions
- Formulas, sorting, fill and eleven chart types; input in Dutch number and date formats
- Notes: notebooks with a free canvas or Markdown pages, saved locally with backups
- Opens MDB and ACCDB without Access in an editable SQLite working copy; the original is never written to
- IMAP and SMTP email client with OAuth2 sign-in for Microsoft and Google
- Save As in about fifteen formats per module; OpenDocument and older Office formats through LibreOffice
- Six separate apps next to the full suite; signed installers for Windows, macOS and Linux

## Tech stack

`TypeScript` · `React` · `Rust` · `Tauri 2` · `SQLite` · `Hunspell`

## When to use this

Local office work on your own computer, without an account or cloud service. Test with copies of your files, because releases are still development builds.
