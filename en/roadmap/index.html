Goal: everything live by end of 2026

We are working hard to have the complete ecosystem production-ready by the end of this year. All tools open source, all data in IFCX.

  • IFCX
    Universal data format for the complete building model
    In development
  • Open PDF Studio
    PDF editor and annotator with a custom Rust engine
    Beta
  • Open 2D Studio
    Open source 2D CAD application
    Beta
  • Open CAD Studio
    2D + 3D CAD in Rust with native DWG/DXF (community project by Hakan Seven)
    Beta
  • Monty IFC Viewer
    Lightweight IFC viewer for the construction site
    Live
  • BIM Validator
    IFC validation against IDS, NL BIM Basis ILS, RVB BIM Norm
    Live
  • Open Calc Studio
    Cost estimation and budgeting (STABU/RAW, IBIS-TRAD import)
    Beta
  • Open Calculations Studio
    Structural calculations with IFCX integration
    Beta
  • Open Frame Studio
    2D frame and structural analysis for buildings and civil load-bearing structures
    Beta
  • Open Heatloss Studio
    Heat loss calculation per NEN 12831 & ISSO 53
    Alpha
  • Open Energy Studio
    Energy analysis and building performance (NTA 8800)
    Beta
  • Open Geotechniek Studio
    CPTs & borings: GEF + BRO-XML viewer, Robertson SBT, map overlays
    Beta
  • Open Field Studio
    Inspection, delivery and quality assurance
    Beta
  • Open Speech Studio
    Speech recognition and meeting notes with local AI
    Beta
  • Open Pointcloud Studio
    Point cloud viewer and as-built comparison
    Alpha
  • Open Planner Studio
    Project planning and coordination
    Beta
  • Y-app
    Communication and collaboration platform (mail, chat, invoicing)
    Beta
  • BCF Manager Studio
    BCF 2.1/3.0 issue management for BIM projects
    Beta
  • OpenAEC Docs
    BIM document management platform
    In development
  • OpenAEC Cloud Platform
    Central platform with SSO and synchronization
    In development
  • BIM Modeller
    Open source BIM modelling tool
    Planned
  • IfcRoad Viewer & Editor
    Visualization and editing of IfcRoad models (roads, intersections, cycle paths) per IFC 4.3 — for civil infrastructure projects
    Planned
  • Open Bridge Studio
    Structural analysis and parametric modelling of bridges and viaducts (IfcBridge) — extension of Open Frame Studio
    Planned
  • Open Hydro Studio
    Hydrological calculations for water works: dikes, flood barriers, sewerage and drainage (IfcMarineFacility)
    Planned

Civil engineering & infrastructure

In 2026/2027 OpenAEC is actively broadening the ecosystem towards ground, road and water works and civil engineering. Alongside the planned tools above, we are building IFCX namespaces for IfcRoad, IfcRail, IfcBridge, IfcTunnel and IfcMarineFacility — so one open format covers both buildings and infrastructure projects.
