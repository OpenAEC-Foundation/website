Features

Six apps in one suite

Start with the highlights or pick a topic.

01 Documents

Edit Word documents directly

Page view, rulers, ribbon and styles. With headers and footers, a table of contents, footnotes, comments, track changes and comparing two versions.

Read more

02 Spreadsheet

Excel files with formulas and charts

Formulas, cell formatting, sorting and fill, with eleven chart types. Input like 1,5, € 1.234,50 or 26-10-2026 is recognised as a number or date.

Read more

03 Presentation

Create and present PowerPoint files

Slides, shapes, text and speaker notes, and a slideshow from the first or current slide.

Read more

04 Notes

Notebooks that stay local

Notebooks, sections and pages, saved locally and automatically, with backups. Each section is a free canvas or a Markdown page with preview, tables and task lists.

Read more

05 Database

Open Access databases without Access

Opens MDB and ACCDB and builds an editable SQLite working copy; the original stays untouched. With tables, queries, simple forms and reports, and a compatibility report per file.

Read more

06 Email

Your email, with no party in between

An IMAP and SMTP client with folders, search and attachments. Office attachments open as an editable copy, and any open document can be sent as an attachment. External images stay blocked until you allow them.

Frequently asked questions

Is it ready for daily work?

Not quite yet. The releases are development releases and not yet signed off for production work. Test with copies of your files and see the known limitations in the release audit on GitHub.

Which files can I open?

DOCX, XLSX and PPTX directly, CSV and TSV, Markdown, and MDB and ACCDB as an editable copy. Documents, spreadsheets and presentations also save and open .ifcx. For OpenDocument and older Office formats, install LibreOffice as well.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Open Office Studio is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence: no licence fees and no account.

Where are my files?

On your own computer. There is no telemetry. Network connections are only for what you set up, such as your mail server. Passwords are kept in your operating system’s keychain.

Do I have to install the whole suite?

No. Documents, spreadsheet, presentation, email, notes and database also come as separate apps. The suite and a separate app each have their own profile; transfer notes with a backup.

Do I need Microsoft Access?

No. Open Database Studio reads MDB and ACCDB itself and works in a SQLite copy; the original file is never written to. Access form designs and macros are inventoried, not executed.

Technology

For those who want to know how it is built.

Tauri 2 and Rust

The desktop shell: files, conversion, email, windows and recovery.

React 19 and TypeScript

The interface with ribbon, editors, charts and printing.

Open document engines

The @betteroffice packages (Apache-2.0) for documents, spreadsheets and presentations.

SQLite and its own VBA interpreter

For Access databases, with a DAO subset.

Hunspell

Spell checking as WebAssembly, with dictionaries from pinned sources.
