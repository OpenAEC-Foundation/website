Speech to text on your own computer

Open Speech Studio

Talking is faster than typing

A desktop application for speech recognition that runs on your own computer. Hold Ctrl+Win and dictate into any application, record a meeting or have audio and video files transcribed. Built on Whisper, recognises 99 languages and runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Features

From voice to text, without the cloud

Start with the highlights or pick a topic.

01 Dictation

Dictate into any application

Hold Ctrl+Win, speak and let go: the text is typed wherever your cursor is. In an email, a chat, a word processor, a browser or a terminal. Rather not hold the keys? Set the hotkey to press once to start and again to stop.

02 Meetings

Record and transcribe meetings

While you record, the meeting is transcribed in pieces, so the transcript grows as you go. For an online meeting the application also records your PC audio on Windows, so the participants in Teams, Zoom or Meet end up in it too. With voice profiles you see who said what; save the transcript as .txt, .md or .odt.

03 Files

Turn audio and video into text

Drop a recording, a voice memo or a video on the window: WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A, MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV and more. Several files are processed side by side. Copy the result, or save it as a text file.

04 Models

Fast or accurate, you choose

Eight Whisper models, from tiny (75 MB) to large-v3 (3.1 GB). The small model is included in the installer, so you can start right away. Small models run smoothly on the processor; with a graphics card (CUDA or Vulkan) the large models are fast too. The language is detected automatically, or you fix it.

05 Read aloud

Have text read aloud

It works the other way round too: paste a text and have it read aloud by a Dutch or English voice. The voices run on the processor of your own computer; each voice is a one-time download of 60 to 110 MB. You set the speed, expressiveness and the pause between sentences yourself. Windows only for now.

  • Your own hotkey

    A combination of two or three keys, to hold down or to switch on and off.

  • Auto paste

    The text appears where your cursor is; if you like, the application presses Enter after it as well.

  • Status window

    A small window shows that it is listening, with the sound level and the progress of the transcription.

  • Sound effects

    A sound on start, stop and done, from a few sound packs and at your own volume.

  • Dictionary

    Teach the model names, abbreviations and jargon, and have words replaced automatically.

  • Auto-correction

    Let a language model on your own computer improve the transcribed text. Off by default.

  • Meetings

    In person with just the microphone, online with your PC audio as well (Windows).

  • Transcribing while recording

    The recording is transcribed every few minutes, or whenever you save a segment.

  • Speaker recognition

    Record a thirty-second voice profile for each person, and the speakers get a label in the transcript.

  • Recording indicator

    A small floating badge stays on top of other windows while recording.

  • Transcribe files

    Audio and video, several at once; double-click for a preview of the result.

  • Export

    Transcripts as .txt, .md or .odt, or saved automatically as text files in a folder of your choice.

  • Eight Whisper models

    From tiny to large-v3, with two compact versions of large-v3-turbo in between.

  • Graphics card or processor

    A graphics card (CUDA or Vulkan) makes it faster; it works without one too.

  • 99 languages

    Detected automatically or set by you, from Dutch and English to Turkish and Japanese.

  • Interface in 25 languages

    Including Dutch, English, French, Spanish and Turkish, switchable in Settings.

  • Microphone test

    Speak a sentence and check that your microphone and the recognition work properly.

  • Text to speech

    Nine Dutch and English voices that run on the processor (Windows).

Hotkeys

Ctrl + Win or Ctrl + Shift + Space

Ctrl+Win works on Windows and on Linux under X11. On macOS and under Wayland you use Ctrl+Shift+Space. In Settings you pick a combination of your own.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. Open Speech Studio is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence. No licence fees, no subscription and no account needed.

Does my voice go to the internet?

No. Recognition runs on your own computer; only models and voices are downloaded once. There is an option to have a remote server do the transcription, but it is off by default and only works after signing in.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows (64-bit), macOS on Apple and Intel processors, and Linux on x86-64 as AppImage, .deb, .rpm or Snap. Text to speech and recording PC audio only work on Windows for now. The Mac app is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID: open it the first time with right-click and Open.

Which hotkey do I use to dictate?

On Windows you hold Ctrl+Win while you speak; Ctrl+Shift+Space works too. On macOS and under Wayland on Linux it is Ctrl+Shift+Space. In Settings you pick your own combination of two or three keys, and whether you hold the keys or press once to start and stop.

Which languages does it recognise?

Whisper recognises 99 languages, including Dutch, English, German, French, Spanish and Turkish. Let it detect the language automatically or fix it. The interface itself is available in 25 languages.

Do I need a graphics card?

No. The smaller models run well on the processor, and the small model is already included in the installer. With a graphics card (CUDA or Vulkan) the large models become fast enough for daily use.

Can I record an online meeting?

Yes, on Windows. Choose an online meeting when you start recording: the application then records your microphone and your PC audio together, so the other participants in Teams, Zoom or Meet end up in the transcript too.

Which files can I have transcribed?

Audio and video, such as WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A, MP4, MKV, AVI and MOV. Drop several at once; they are processed side by side and you save the result as a text file.

Technology and licence

For those who want to know how it is built.

whisper.cpp

The Whisper models run through whisper.cpp, on the processor or on a graphics card with CUDA or Vulkan.

Tauri 2 and Rust

A desktop application with a Rust core for recording, recognition and files, for Windows, macOS and Linux.

SolidJS

The interface is built with SolidJS and Vite, in the style of the OpenAEC desktop apps.

Audio

Recording through cpal; on Windows the PC audio is mixed with the microphone for online meetings.

Piper

Text to speech with Piper: small voice models that run on the processor without a graphics card or server.

Apache-2.0

Free to use, modify and distribute. The source code is on GitHub, with the issues for a problem or a request.
