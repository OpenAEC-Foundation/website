Open Field Studio

Free & open-source tool for inspection, handover and quality assurance in construction projects.

Beta
v0.1.3 · 18 commits · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Tauri v2

Why Open Field Studio?

Inspections and handovers on the construction site require reliable tools that also work offline. Open Field Studio combines checklists, defect registration and reporting in an app that works fully offline and is available in four languages.

Features

Tickets & defects on floor plan

Register defects and remarks directly on the floor plan. Visual overview of all open items per location.

15 checklist templates (NEN 2767)

Ready-made checklists conforming to NEN 2767 and other standards. Customizable to project-specific requirements.

Energy label survey (NTA 8800)

Perform energy label surveys conforming to NTA 8800 directly on-site with structured input forms.

Monument inspection

Specific inspection modules for heritage buildings with attention to cultural-historical values.

Handover with signatures

Digital handover reports with signature functionality. All parties sign directly on screen.

Dashboard with live statistics

Real-time overview of all inspections, open defects and progress per project.

Camera integration

Take photos directly from the app and link them to defects, checklists or locations on the floor plan.

4 languages (NL/EN/DE/FR)

Fully available in Dutch, English, German and French. Ideal for international projects.

Fully offline

Works without internet connection on the construction site. Sync when you are back online.

Professional report export

Export inspections and handovers as professional PDF reports with photos and floor plans.
