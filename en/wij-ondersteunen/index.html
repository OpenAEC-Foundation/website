OpenAEC Foundation

Projects we support

The OpenAEC ecosystem stands on the shoulders of open source. The projects below are an essential part of everything we build — which is why we support them and contribute back.

Open source is not a free raw material. Behind every framework, calculation library and standard our tools rely on are people and communities who maintain it. Without them there is no Open PDF Studio, no Open CAD Studio, no SuperCloud.

That is why a share of what OpenAEC receives flows back to the projects we build on — through donations, sponsorship and contributions in code. What we open up we do not keep to ourselves, and what we use we do not let wither.

01

Give back, not just take

Whoever builds on open source should contribute to maintaining it.

02

Safeguard sovereignty

Healthy upstream projects keep us independent of vendor lock-in.

03

Open standards first

We invest in the open standards that move the whole sector forward.
