OpenAEC Open Speech Studio vs Dragon

Open Speech Studio is a free, open source alternative to Dragon NaturallySpeaking. Compare speech recognition, dictation, languages, platforms and privacy — factual and balanced.

Dragon NaturallySpeaking (Nuance, part of Microsoft since 2022) has been the established standard for desktop speech-to-text for decades. Dragon Professional typically costs €500-700 one-time, and there is a subscription variant (Dragon Anywhere) at around €15-20 per month. The software runs on Windows only, processes audio 100% locally, and has a strong reputation for dictation accuracy and industry-specific vocabularies (medical, legal).

Open Speech Studio from the OpenAEC Foundation is a free, open source speech recognition application built on Whisper AI from OpenAI. It runs cross-platform (Windows, macOS, Linux), processes audio 100% locally, and supports 99 languages out-of-the-box. License: LGPL-3.0. No subscription, no telemetry, no vendor lock-in.

Honest positioning: Dragon has decades of experience with dictation accuracy and industry-specific vocabularies (medical, legal) and extensive voice profile training. Open Speech Studio uses state-of-the-art Whisper AI, which performs excellently for general dictation, but is a younger project without the specialized industry dictionaries Dragon has built up over decades.

When should you choose which?

Choose Open Speech Studio if...

  • You don't want to pay €500-700 and don't want a subscription either.
  • You work on macOS or Linux (Dragon is Windows-only).
  • You need multiple languages — Whisper supports 99 languages out-of-the-box.
  • You don't want vendor lock-in and want to be able to inspect the source code.
  • You value transparency and want to support open source.
  • You already use other OpenAEC tools and want integrated workflows.
  • You value privacy and data sovereignty — everything runs locally, no telemetry.

Choose Dragon NaturallySpeaking if...

  • You need advanced voice profile training for maximum personal accuracy.
  • You work in a specific field with extensive vocabularies (medical, legal) that Dragon has spent decades optimizing for.
  • You need 24/7 enterprise support with an SLA.
  • You have legacy Dragon macros you don't want to lose that are deeply embedded in your workflow.
  • You work exclusively on Windows and the license cost isn't an issue.
  • You need commercial vendor accountability for compliance reasons.

Download for Windows, macOS or Linux and experience Whisper AI speech recognition in 99 languages — local, private and without a subscription. No registration, no account, no telemetry.
