OpenAEC vs Bluebeam

Open PDF Studio is a free, open source alternative to Bluebeam Revu. Compare PDF annotation, measurement tools, redaction and collaboration — factual and balanced.

Bluebeam Revu has been the de facto industry standard for PDF markup in construction since 2002. The software offers advanced annotation, measurement and collaboration features and is deeply embedded in workflows at contractors, architects and engineering firms. Bluebeam Revu typically costs €300-500 per user per year (subscription), depending on edition (Basics, Core, Complete) and country.

Open PDF Studio from the OpenAEC Foundation is a free, open source PDF editor with its own Rust engine for fast vector PDF rendering. The project now has more than 15.858 downloads and offers 20+ annotation tools, measurement functions, redaction, OCR and forms. License: LGPL-3.0. No subscription, no telemetry, no vendor lock-in.

When do you choose which?

Choose Open PDF Studio if...

  • You don't want to pay an annual subscription for PDF markup.
  • You work cross-platform (Linux, macOS and Android are fully supported).
  • Privacy and data sovereignty matter to you — no telemetry, everything local.
  • You're a freelancer or small firm who needs professional PDF tools fast, without license management.
  • You want to adapt or integrate the software into your own workflow (LGPL-3.0).
  • You want to open vector PDFs (technical drawings) blazing fast thanks to the Rust engine.
  • You want to support open source in the AEC sector.

Choose Bluebeam Revu if...

  • You collaborate intensively in Bluebeam Studio with external parties who also use Bluebeam.
  • You need specific Bluebeam plug-ins or integrations already embedded in your workflow.
  • You work at an organization where Bluebeam is the company standard and you share tool chests.
  • You need advanced calibration and take-off workflows that are already set up within your team.
  • You need commercial 24/7 vendor support with an SLA.
  • License cost isn't an issue and you prefer the most refined feature set.
