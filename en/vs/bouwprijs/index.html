OpenAEC vs Bouwprijs

Open Calc Studio is an open source Dutch construction cost calculator with STABU/RAW support and AI integration via MCP. Compare to Bouwprijs and IBIS-Trad.

Bouwprijs (Sdu/Bouwformatie) and IBIS-Trad have been established names in Dutch construction cost estimation for decades. They offer extensive reference prices, STABU specification linking, RAW specification systematics, and historical project data. Prices vary significantly by edition and organization size, but for a single workstation they typically range from a few hundred to a few thousand euros per year (including a data subscription).

Open Calc Studio (v0.10.0) from the OpenAEC Foundation is an open source construction cost calculator built for the Dutch market. It supports STABU and RAW, offers hierarchical calculations, bills of quantities, and cost estimates, and is unique in its category: a built-in MCP server (Model Context Protocol) lets you drive the calculation directly from AI assistants such as Claude.

Unique: AI integration

Open Calc Studio as MCP server

Open Calc Studio is one of the first Dutch construction cost tools to support the Model Context Protocol (MCP). This lets an AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT with MCP, etc.) build up calculations, enter quantities, validate rules, or run analyses on its own — without you having to type everything by hand. A fundamental shift in how construction cost estimation works: from manual entry to AI-assisted estimating, auditable and open.

When should you choose which?

Choose Open Calc Studio when...

  • You want to experiment with AI-assisted estimating via MCP — a unique combination of calculation and AI.
  • You don't want to take out an annual subscription or data agreement.
  • You work cross-platform (macOS and Linux are supported).
  • As a freelancer, cost estimator, or small firm, you want to produce STABU or RAW calculations.
  • You want to add your own reference prices or rules and manage them yourself.
  • You're willing to work with a tool in active development (v0.10.0) and want to contribute yourself.
  • You want to support open source and open standards in the Dutch construction sector.

Choose Bouwprijs / IBIS-Trad when...

  • You rely daily on extensive, validated reference prices for the Dutch market.
  • You need access to historical project data spanning decades of calculations.
  • You work in an organization where Bouwprijs or IBIS-Trad is the standard and your team is already trained on it.
  • You need commercial support and data maintenance with an SLA.
  • You use specific STABU and RAW integrations that are deeply embedded in your tendering workflow.
  • Stability and feature completeness matter more to you than open source and AI innovation.
