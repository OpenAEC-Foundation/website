In development

OpenAEC SuperCloud

SuperCloud brings Frappe, a CDE, document management and IFCX together in one environment for the entire construction project. The product is in development: currently with 6 major customers, opening worldwide on 1 January.

This week: 8 improvements to the infrastructure.

  • Video calls with Jitsi Meet in every environment
  • The backup now also includes the BIM platform of each environment
  • Sign in with Apple prepared in the deployment
  • BIM platform previews are retried when they fail
  • External apps configurable per environment at build time

By project

What is new in each tool

Open BIM Suite

98 improvements · 6 accounts · +25,479 lines · 6 issues resolved · 1 release

  • Draw reinforcement in the Modeller: meshes, strips and individual bars
  • Export sheets in one go as a series or as a single booklet
  • Fill in the title block directly on the sheet
  • Select, trim and lock all grids at once
  • Validator: a clash matrix per class, with tolerance and weight
  • Validator: classification and views as rules in the rule set
  • Align models on two pairs of points and replace them with a new revision
  • Rendering a frame of the interface takes 3 ms instead of 42 on a large model
  • The BIM Validator gets its own installer

Open Stair Studio

17 improvements · 1 account · +1,240 lines · 13 releases

  • New this week: the first version, with a signed updater
  • Design, calculate and prepare stairs: workshop drawings, cost estimates, CNC and IFC4
  • Housed stringers with a flat bearing foot and the true pentagonal cross-section
  • Straight steel stairs with an open triangular plate stringer
  • Stairwells over multiple storeys, with precast concrete stairs
  • Thirteen releases in one week

Open Office Studio

56 improvements · 2 accounts · +67,929 lines · 6 issues resolved · 2 releases

  • Release 2026.48: remove duplicates, text to columns, goal seek and descriptive statistics
  • Spell checking in cells, in the cell editor and on slides
  • Keyboard shortcuts as in other spreadsheets: Ctrl+arrow, Ctrl+End and Alt+Enter
  • Building blocks, an AutoCorrect dialog and a Navigator in Writer
  • Faster in large Writer documents and built-in file conversion on the desktop

Open Geotechniek Studio

12 improvements · 1 account · +4,906 lines · 2 issues resolved · 2 releases

  • Create a CPT from a scanned, old CPT chart
  • Bearing capacity table with a governing overview and a site plan of the CPTs
  • Multiple pile types and CPTs without a position in the overview
  • Monitoring wells per aquifer in the 3D model
  • Export the bearing capacity table for Pile Plan Studio
  • Read dissipation tests from GEF files

Pile Plan Studio

4 improvements · 1 account · +6,026 lines · 1 release

  • First stable desktop version for Windows, macOS and Linux
  • Export pile plans to CAD and PDF
  • Existing project files continue to open
  • Now also available through the OpenAEC Installer

Open Planner Studio

682 improvements · 1 account · +81,222 lines · 9 issues resolved · 1 release

  • Pick dates from a custom calendar, with week numbers and keyboard control
  • Collapse stacked milestones into a single row
  • A custom bar colour for each type of work
  • Saving computes in the background, with the status in the status bar
  • Schedules in IFCX with resources, assignments and baselines (in development)

Open CalcSheet Studio

51 improvements · 2 accounts · +34,381 lines · 26 issues resolved · 2 releases

  • Export the report as editable DOCX or ODT
  • A customisable report template, also as a separate file
  • Standards per country: the report lists the standards that apply there
  • Choose between the first and second generation Eurocodes per project
  • Every calculation sheet also on the canvas
  • Link input to a cell from a spreadsheet or workbook
  • Lithuanian as a new language

Open Calc Studio

27 improvements · 2 accounts · +12,835 lines · 5 issues resolved · 1 release

  • Custom price libraries from JSON, CSV, Excel and BMEcat side by side
  • Choose a country with its matching library for each estimate
  • Measurement lines under estimate items, with BC3 import and export
  • Can be embedded as a component in your own website

Open Frame Studio

9 improvements · 1 account · +13,709 lines · 1 issues resolved · 5 releases

  • Twenty workspaces in one window, from 2D and 3D to cutting plan, energy and certification
  • A view cube and a section in the 3D viewer, showing how sash, rebate and seals fit together
  • Cavity batten adjustable for each side of the frame
  • Workshop drawing with free panel dimensions and the centre positions of jambs and rails
  • Production lists, cutting optimisation, circularity and an indicative pre-check against standards
  • A new demo film, also on YouTube
  • Five releases in one week

Open PDF Studio

Open Infra Studio
