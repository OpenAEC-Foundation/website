Historical building literature

OpenBooks

Old building textbooks, free online

Anyone who renovates, restores or assesses an existing building needs to know how it was built back then. That knowledge sits in textbooks that have been out of print for decades. OpenBooks scans them and puts them online for free: leaf through the original pages and zoom in on the drawings, without an account.

Frequently asked questions

What does OpenBooks cost?

Nothing. You need no account or subscription: open the site and start reading.

Can these books simply be put online?

We only publish books that, as far as we can tell, are free of copyright. With old books that is not always easy to establish. If a book is still under copyright after all, let us know at info@open-aec.com and we will take it down right away.

Are all books in the reader?

Not yet. The reader now shows thirteen books. The scans of the other books, such as the remaining volumes of Wattjes and Vandepitte, are already on GitHub and are being added step by step.

Can I search the text of the books?

Not yet. For now you search by title and author. The text of a few books has already been recognised with OCR; searching the text of all books is planned.

What language are the books in?

Almost all are in Dutch; there is one German chapter. The website itself is in Dutch too.

I have an old textbook. Can it be added?

Gladly. Suggest the book on GitHub, with its title, author and year. The wish list includes the other books by Wattjes, older concrete standards and old building magazines.

May I reuse pages?

The scans are there to be used: in a report, a renovation plan or a lesson. Please credit the book and its author as the source. All scans are available as separate images on GitHub.
