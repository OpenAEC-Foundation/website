Open Infra Studio

Open-source GIS and civil design, right inside your CAD drawing

GIS and civil design straight into your drawing, wherever in the world your project is. Choose the project location, bring in map services, OpenStreetMap and 3D city models, and design alignments, profiles and corridors. For the Netherlands, BGT, cadastre and KLIC come on top, on NLCS layers with object data and a complete drawing sheet.

Open standards

Built on open standards

An infrastructure project lasts for decades, and so must its data. That is why Open Infra Studio works with open formats and open services, and writes the whole drawing as IFC 4.3. The direction is IFCX: the next generation of the IFC format, open and extensible, in which the data of every discipline is linked to one model.

What is IFCX? The IFC 4.3 export

  • Today: IFC 4.3

    The whole drawing as IFC4X3_ADD2: alignments as IfcAlignment, and terrain, pavement, cables, pipes and earthworks each with their own IFC class, georeferenced and with their object data.

  • The direction: IFCX

    IFCX builds on IFC 4.3, which also covers roads, rail, cables and pipes, and it is the course of the OpenAEC Foundation for the whole ecosystem. Today Open Infra Studio writes IFC 4.3, not yet IFCX.

  • Open data, open services

    WMS, WFS, OGC API Features, CityGML, CityJSON, LandXML and GeoPackage, without API keys and without being tied to one vendor.

IFC 4.3 now, IFCX as the direction

The direction after that is IFCX, the open and extensible next generation of IFC.

05 Automation

Scriptable and ready for AI

Every command takes arguments and returns its result as JSON, with handles, lengths and volumes. An MCP server lets an AI assistant build whole models, from project location to corridor, and finds every Open CAD Studio window by itself.

Read more

  • Project location

    Click the map or search an address, place, parcel or coordinate pair: worldwide through OpenStreetMap, and in the Netherlands also through PDOK, with suggestions as you type. Choose the coordinate system, draw the import area and choose absolute or relative coordinates. The location is stored in the drawing itself.

  • Any coordinate system

    14 built in, including RD New, ETRS89 / UTM, Belgian Lambert, British National Grid and Lambert-93. The right UTM zone automatically anywhere in the world, and any other EPSG code is fetched once and kept with the drawing.

  • Map services worldwide

    A built-in catalogue of more than 600 checked public WMS, WFS and tile services in 45 countries, without API keys. Search by place or theme, in other languages too, see which services cover the project location and load a layer, reprojected to the project's coordinate system.

  • Aerial photos and maps

    Choose a WMS or TMS server, browse its layers and preview them on the map. The images go behind the drawing as underlays. 19 server presets for the Netherlands, Flanders and the rest of the world, plus your own servers.

  • WFS vector data

    Choose a server and a feature type, test it on the map, then choose the label attribute, the colour and clipping to the import area.

  • OpenStreetMap, worldwide

    Buildings, roads, rail, water, civil structures, land use, cables and pipelines, fences and house numbers, anywhere in the world.

  • CityGML and CityJSON

    3D city models from anywhere: CityGML 1.0 to 3.0, CityJSON 1.x and 2.0 and CityJSONSeq. The file is reprojected to the project location and read object by object, keeping only the project area; a 97 MB city tile loads in under two seconds.

  • Terrain with its photo, worldwide

    The ground of any project location as a 3D surface coloured by its photo: heights from AHN in the Netherlands and from open worldwide height data elsewhere.

  • From point cloud to terrain

    A TIN from the AHN points of the project area or a LAS, LAZ, COPC, E57, PLY, PTS or XYZ file, ground lines along an alignment, a ground filter for scans and a 3D mesh of an object such as a bridge.

  • Import QGIS connections

    Read a WMS, WFS or XYZ connections file exported from QGIS, check the preview and add the chosen connections in one go. Passwords are never read.

  • Regional profiles

    The Netherlands, the United States and International, each with its own data menu. The region is stored per drawing and is independent of the language.

  • The Civiel tab

    A second ribbon tab for civil design: survey points and LandXML, a TIN with contours and height analysis, alignments with stationing, length, design and cross sections, and corridors with daylight to the ground, earthworks and a mass balance.

  • Alignments and rail

    Exactly computed clothoids, design criteria per guideline (ROA, NOA, AASHTO), an alignment check, sight distance, and rail tracks with cant and turnouts.

  • Corridors and junctions

    A parts library with kerbs, footways, cycle paths, ditches and pavement layers, your own assemblies as JSON, and junctions and roundabouts.

  • Grading

    Feature lines, plateaus and slopes into one design surface, exact TIN-to-TIN volumes and a mass haul diagram.

  • Survey and site

    Survey codes become linework and symbols. Divide plots into parcels, and add water drops, catchments, ponds, reverse swept paths and parking bays.

  • Sewers

    Manholes and pipes along a line, with invert levels from minimum cover and slope, a length profile and checks on cover, slope, velocity and KLIC crossings. Export to GWSW-OroX, GWSW-HydX and CSV.

  • Plan production

    Sheet sets along an alignment, each a layout with the plan strip and length profile, and every sheet as PDF. Labels for stationing, curve data, grades and heights follow their source.

  • Export to IFC 4.3

    The whole drawing as IFC4X3_ADD2. Every object gets its IFC class from its NLCS layer and object data, from terrain, buildings and pavements to cables and pipes, CPTs with their soil layers, earthworks and the corridor as IfcRoad. Axes become an IfcAlignment with horizontal and vertical layout. Georeferenced with IfcMapConversion, with object data and NLCS layer as property sets, and checked with IfcOpenShell. Corridors with pavement courses and earthwork bodies, sewers with invert levels and point-cloud meshes come along too.

  • LandXML 1.2

    Read points, TINs and alignments, and write points, TINs, alignments with profiles and corridor feature lines, checked against the schema.

  • GeoPackage and GeoJSON

    Every element with its layer and object data back to GIS: as a GeoPackage in the project coordinate system or as GeoJSON in WGS 84.

  • Scripts and AI agents

    The commands take arguments and write their result as JSON, with handles, lengths and volumes. An MCP server lets an AI agent build whole models, from project location to corridor, and finds every Open CAD Studio window by itself.

For the Netherlands

Dutch base data and NLCS, built in

Everything that only works in the Netherlands sits in one group in the ribbon: PDOK, BGT, cadastre, KLIC, AHN, BRO and the NLCS drawing standard. Working elsewhere? Switch that group off with one setting.

NL Base data

BGT, cadastre and KLIC on NLCS layers

Fetch the BGT, the cadastral map and a KLIC delivery for the project area. Every object lands on the right NLCS layer through the official NLCS-BGT mapping, with its source attributes as object data.

NL Drawing sheet

A complete NLCS drawing sheet

Frame, title block listing the sources of the imports, legend of the layers used, north arrow, scale bar and RD crosses, on the paper size that fits. Five commands put the existing situation on paper.

NL Subsurface

Cables, pipes and CPTs in 3D

KLIC cables and pipes as tubes at their depth, and BRO cone penetration tests and boreholes as columns of soil layers, under a see-through ground surface. All of it goes along to IFC 4.3.

  • BGT to NLCS

    42 PDOK collections in 11 groups, current objects only. Each object goes to the most specific rule of the official NLCS-BGT 5.0 mapping (719 rules); street names and house numbers keep their BGT rotation.

  • Cadastral map

    Parcel boundaries and parcel numbers from the BRK, the numbers at their BRK rotation, in one click on the NLCS layer for cadastral boundaries.

  • KLIC deliveries

    Open the Kadaster's delivery, as a zip or as the area-information XML. 13 IMKL 2.0 themes go on their own layers, and cables and pipes are labelled per network operator with diameter and material. Areas with a required precaution get a red hatch, and you open the attachments of the delivery from the window.

  • Prepare a KLIC notification

    An orientation request, excavation notification or emergency notification for a drawn polyline, checked locally against the Kadaster rules. You submit it yourself. New deliveries in your downloads folder are drawn in automatically.

  • The subsurface in 3D

    Cables and pipes from a KLIC delivery as tubes at their depth, or at the usual cover depth when the delivery gives none. BRO cone penetration tests and boreholes become columns of soil layers. A see-through ground shows it all from below.

  • Geotechnics from the BRO

    Soil profiles from CPTs and boreholes, slope stability with Bishop or Fellenius and settlement with Koppejan or Terzaghi, as a calculation aid.

  • PDOK in one click

    Aerial photo, cadastral map, AHN, BAG, parcels, NWB road sections, hectometre markers, ProRail tracks and waterways, without opening a window, at the saved location.

  • Historical maps

    Every Dutch topographic map from 1815 to 2014 (Topotijdreis) and the cadastral maps of 1832 (HisGIS) as underlays, next to Natura 2000, the geomorphological map and the soil map.

  • NLCS 5.02 built in

    3,659 objects, 543 linetypes and 256 colours. Each layer gets the NLCS colour, lineweight and linetype of the chosen drawing status; linetypes are in paper millimetres and follow the plot scale.

  • NLCS++ for network operators

    Read deliveries of the network operators (V11 up to V12.1) onto their layers, check them and deliver them again as V12.1 XML, valid against the operator’s XSD.

  • Drawing sheet

    Frame, title block listing the sources of the imports, legend of the NLCS layers used, north arrow, scale bar and RD crosses. The format, A3 to A0 landscape, is chosen automatically.

  • BGT mutation

    The design on NLCS layers goes back to the BGT, as IMGeo-GML 2.1.1 with a report beside the drawing. The file is valid against the IMGeo 2.1.1 schema; anything ambiguous is listed in the report instead of exported.

  • Object data

    Every object keeps its source attributes: from the BGT its local id, function and material, from KLIC the network operator, diameter and material.

  • Infra library

    94 parametric NLCS objects, from pavements, kerbs and pipes to manholes, cabinets, lighting and green, with smart connections.

  • Spatial plans

    Search the plans at a location through the Dutch Ruimtelijke Plannen API, with your own key, or read a local IMRO GML, drawn with the official map styles.

Install

Download v2026.46.0

Open Infra Studio is a plugin for Open CAD Studio and comes in two parts: the GIS plugin and the Civiel plugin. The latest release needs Open CAD Studio v2026.44, so install or update that first.

  1. 1

    Install Open CAD Studio v2026.44 Download Open CAD Studio from its product page, or update your installation to v2026.44, and start it.

  2. 2

    GIS plugin through the Plugin Manager Type PLUGINS, pick Open Infra Studio from the list, choose the release and click Install. In an older Open CAD Studio, choose Add a repository and enter OpenAEC-Foundation/Open-Infra-Studio.

  3. 3

    Unzip the Civiel plugin Download open-infra-civiel-<platform>.zip below and unzip it into <config>/OpenCADStudio/plugins/. That gives the folder openaec.open-infra-civiel.

  4. 4

    Restart Restart Open CAD Studio. The ribbon then shows the tabs GIS and Civiel.

Unzip the zip file for your system into <config>/OpenCADStudio/plugins/. It holds the folder openaec.open-infra-civiel with the library and its own plugin.toml. Then restart Open CAD Studio.

<config> is %APPDATA% on Windows, ~/.config on Linux and ~/Library/Application Support on macOS.

The libraries are signed: on Windows with Authenticode and a timestamp (publisher Impertio Studio B.V.), on macOS with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, and on Linux with a detached OpenPGP signature (.asc) of the OpenAEC Foundation. The public key openaec-signing-public-key.asc is in the release; its fingerprint is 13F6D414ADEE4ED4CD09AC60601D86C93C504D29.

New in v2026.46.0

Geotechnics and safer updates

Released on 9 November 2026, for Open CAD Studio v2026.44. Besides the new geotechnics, most changes make large drawings and awkward files safer to work with.

Full changelog Install

  • Geotechnics

    Soil profiles from BRO CPTs and boreholes, slip circles with Bishop or Fellenius and settlement with Koppejan or Terzaghi. Calculation aids, not a stamped design.

  • Sections follow their source

    Cross sections read BGT, KLIC and survey points on large drawings too, and are marked when a source changes. When updating, the old geometry stays if a calculation fails.

  • NLCS++

    Relation objects without geometry are kept on import, and references are rewritten correctly per delivery.

  • Cleaner export

    HydX gets unique manhole and pipe IDs across networks; GeoJSON puts only longitude and latitude in the coordinates and the height in a property of its own.

  • More robust reading

    Deeply nested XML and extreme spirals in LandXML no longer hang the plugin, and every command stays with its own drawing when you switch tabs.

  • MCP over HTTP with a token

    Outside your own computer the MCP server asks for a token and checks the host, origin and content type of every request.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. Open Infra Studio is open source under the GPL-3.0: no licence fees, no subscription and no account. Open CAD Studio, which the plugin runs in, is free too.

Which version of Open CAD Studio do I need?

The latest release belongs to Open CAD Studio v2026.44. A plugin for another plugin API does not load. When you update Open CAD Studio, update the plugin too; the version table shows what belongs together.

Does it work outside the Netherlands?

Yes. The worldwide parts come first: project location and coordinate systems, the catalogue of map services in 45 countries, OpenStreetMap, terrain with its aerial photo and the whole civil design. There are regional profiles for the Netherlands, the United States and International.

Which language is the plugin in?

The interface is in English or Dutch and follows Open CAD Studio's language, falling back to English. Next to the language choice you can switch off the Dutch modules; after a restart the Netherlands group and the 3D Netherlands menu are gone from the ribbon.

Does it work on macOS?

The ribbon quick buttons and the commands work; the workbench window does not yet.

How accurate is the conversion to RD?

Conversion to RD uses the 7-parameter transformation, about 0.2 metres from RDNAPTRANS. That is enough for underlays and context. For setting out, use data that is already in RD; PDOK, BGT, BRK and KLIC all supply it that way.

May I use the data it fetches?

Data from PDOK, the Kadaster, Rijkswaterstaat, ProRail, AHN and the BRO is open data; credit the source, which the title block does for the imports. NLCS and the 3D BAG are CC BY 4.0, OpenStreetMap is ODbL. The Sentinel-2 satellite background is for non-commercial use only.

Can I open IFC or IFCX?

Not yet. Open Infra Studio writes the drawing as IFC 4.3, but does not read IFC. IFCX is the direction for the future and is not in the plugin yet.

Does it work with an AI assistant?

Yes. Every command takes arguments and answers in JSON, and an MCP server with some thirty tools lets an AI assistant build whole models, from project location to corridor. Outside your own computer the server asks for a token.

Technology

For those who want to know how it is built and which formats go in and out.

Rust egui proj4rs OGC API Features CityGML / CityJSON LandXML IFC 4.3 IMKL 2.0 NLCS 5.02 MCP GPL-3.0

Written in Rust, in one Cargo workspace with the GIS plugin, the Civiel plugin and a separate NLCS++ library. A core with no dependency on the CAD host handles coordinates, servers, parsing and the civil calculations; a separate layer turns the result into DWG entities. Builds for Windows, Linux (x86-64 and ARM64) and macOS (Apple silicon).

File formats

Open CAD Studio itself opens and saves the drawing (DWG and DXF, R13 to 2018) and plots it to PDF. Open Infra Studio adds the formats below. Imported files are reprojected to the project location, using the file's own coordinate system where it has one.

Open

  • Vector GIS GeoJSON, Shapefile, GeoPackage, KML and KMZ, GPX, GML, and CSV, TXT or TSV with coordinate columns
  • Rasters and height grids GeoTIFF, ASCII grid (.asc), JPEG and PNG with a world file
  • Point clouds LAS and LAZ; for a terrain in the Civiel tab also COPC, E57, PLY, PTS and XYZ
  • Survey points and LandXML PNEZD, PENZD, NEZD and ENZD; LandXML 1.x with points, surfaces and alignments
  • 3D city models CityGML 1.0 to 3.0, IMGeo/BGT GML, CityJSON 1.x and 2.0, CityJSONSeq
  • KLIC delivery IMKL 2.0, as a zip or as XML
  • Spatial plans IMRO GML
  • Server connections WMS, WFS and XYZ connections from QGIS

Write

  • IFC 4.3 the whole drawing as IFC4X3_ADD2, through GIS → Output → IFC 4x3 or Civiel → 3D model → IFC 4x3 on the ribbon, with GIS_IFC or CIV_IFC [BESTAND=] [SELECTIE] [TEKST=nee], or with the MCP tool ifc_export
  • LandXML 1.2 points, TINs, alignments with profiles and corridor feature lines, checked against the schema
  • Geodata every element with its layer and object data as a GeoPackage in the project coordinate system or as GeoJSON in WGS 84
  • Sewers GWSW-OroX, GWSW-HydX and CSV
  • Sheets every sheet of a sheet set as PDF
  • Map images and point clouds PNG beside the drawing; point clouds as .rcs, attached to the drawing
  • Tables survey points, the mass balance per corridor and quantities as CSV, quantities also as a table in the drawing
  • BGT mutation IMGeo-GML 2.1.1 with a report
  • NLCS check a report in Markdown
  • Command results JSON per call, for scripts and agents

Web services

  • Maps WMS 1.1.1 and 1.3.0, XYZ and TMS tiles, and tile caches such as Topotijdreis's
  • Vector data WFS 1.1.0 and 2.0.0, OGC API Features, the Overpass API for OpenStreetMap
  • Heights and 3D WCS for height grids, COPC for point clouds, the 3D BAG API
  • Other BRO REST for cone penetration tests and boreholes, address search through PDOK and Nominatim, epsg.io for coordinate systems

Not yet: reading JPEG 2000 and IFC, and exporting to Shapefile.
