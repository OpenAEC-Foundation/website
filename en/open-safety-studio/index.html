Open Safety Studio

Health and safety plan, BLVC plan and risks in one app

Draw up a health and safety (V&G) plan, a BLVC plan, a risk matrix or a safe maintenance plan, always with the relevant legal article alongside. Start from a template for your type of project, with risks and measures ready to go, and turn it into a report.

Features

From risk to plan

Start with the highlights or pick a topic.

01 Risk matrix

See which risk needs attention first

Rate every risk on probability and severity in a five-by-five matrix. The score sets the level, from very low to very high, and the colour in the matrix shows at once where the problems are. For each risk you record the measures, each with a person responsible.

02 Health and safety plan

Everything Article 2.28 asks for, in one place

Project details, parties involved, phases, the special risks from Annex II, asbestos, hazardous substances, emergency procedures and the worksite arrangement. The sidebar shows what the Dutch Working Conditions Decree (Arbobesluit) requires and when a health and safety plan is mandatory.

03 BLVC plan

The building site and its surroundings

Record per phase how the area stays accessible, how you limit nuisance, how passers-by stay safe and how you inform local residents: from traffic diversions and working hours to fencing, signage and a complaints procedure.

04 Safe maintenance

A building that can be maintained safely

Fill in the building details, go through the safe maintenance checklist and keep track of fixed facilities such as anchor points and roof edge protection, with their last and next inspection. The basis is Article 6.53 of the Dutch Buildings Decree (Bouwbesluit) and Article 2.30 of the Arbobesluit.

  • Health and safety plan

    All parts of Article 2.28 of the Arbobesluit, from project details to emergency procedures.

  • Parties involved

    The client, the health and safety coordinators for design and execution, and the contractors.

  • Asbestos and hazardous substances

    A reference to the asbestos inventory, and for each substance the risk, the measure and whether a safety data sheet is available.

  • Emergency procedures

    The emergency response team, the first aid post, the evacuation route, the assembly point and the emergency number.

  • BLVC plan

    Accessibility, liveability, safety and communication, per phase of the project.

  • Safe maintenance

    A ten-point checklist and a list of fixed facilities with their status and inspection dates.

  • The legal basis in view

    Each part shows which article applies and when a plan is mandatory.

  • Ten sector templates

    From housing and high-rise to demolition, civil works, hydraulic engineering, installation work and foundation work.

  • Twenty risk modules

    Together 37 risks with 124 measures, from falls from height to confined spaces and lifting.

  • A 5 × 5 risk matrix

    Probability times severity gives a score and a level from very low to very high.

  • Measures in the right order

    Following the occupational hygiene strategy: the source first, then technical and organisational measures, personal protective equipment last.

  • Eighteen categories

    From falls and electricity to asbestos, noise, excavation, drowning and weather conditions.

  • The health and safety plan as a report

    A formatted document with references to Articles 2.26 to 2.31 of the Arbobesluit.

  • Preview with zoom

    View the report in the app first, or open it in a window of its own.

  • Print or PDF

    Print the report or save it as a PDF through the print function.

  • In the browser

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Open Safety Studio yet?

You can try it in the browser. It is still in development: saving and reopening projects is not possible yet, and there is no release.

What does it cost?

Nothing. You use the app for free and without an account.

When is a health and safety plan mandatory?

Under the Arbobesluit, among other things when special risks from Annex II apply, when the work takes more than thirty working days with more than twenty people at the same time, or when it takes more than five hundred person-days. The app shows this next to the plan.

Which rules does it cover?

The Dutch ones: Articles 2.26 to 2.31 of the Arbobesluit for the health and safety plan, and Article 6.53 of the Bouwbesluit for safe maintenance. Always check the current regulations for your own project.

How do the templates work?

Choose a template for your type of project, such as renovation of occupied homes or foundation work. The template sets up the matching risk modules, with risks and measures that you then adapt to your project.

What language is the app in?

The interface is in Dutch and English. The plans themselves follow Dutch regulations.

Is the source code available?

Not publicly yet. You can use the app itself for free, and share ideas and problems with the feedback button.
