Open Safety Studio

Open source tool for safety analysis and risk assessment on construction projects.

Beta

Why Open Safety Studio?

Safety on the construction site is not a luxury but an obligation. Open Safety Studio helps with creating risk inventories, health & safety plans and safety checks, linked to the BIM model so risks become spatially visible.

Features

Risk inventory

Systematic inventory of safety risks per construction phase, location and type of work in accordance with occupational health and safety legislation.

H&S plan generation

Automatically generate Health & Safety plans based on the risk inventory and project data.

Safety inspections

Digital checklists for safety inspections on the construction site. Register deviations and track corrective actions.

IFCX connection

Link risks and safety measures to specific locations and elements in the BIM model via IFCX.

Incident registration

Register incidents, near-misses and unsafe situations. Analyze patterns and take preventive measures.

Reporting & compliance

Generate reports for internal audits and external supervisors. Prove compliance with laws and regulations.
