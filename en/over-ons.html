Why OpenAEC?

The AEC industry is one of the last major industries that still largely runs on closed, proprietary software. We want to change that.

The OpenAEC Foundation develops, supports and promotes open source software for the entire AEC chain (buildings, infrastructure, civil engineering) — from design to handover and management. Our goal: make the entire AEC industry open source this year.

Transparency

Open code, open data, open processes. Everyone can look, think along and build together.

Collaboration

We believe the best software is created when the entire sector works together, not competes on tools.

Accessibility

No license costs, no vendor lock-in. Professional tools for everyone in construction.
