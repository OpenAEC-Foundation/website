Blender + Bonsai

Third-party open-source app, hosted or connected by OpenAEC. Its makers are not affiliated with OpenAEC.

About this app

Bonsai turns Blender into a full BIM environment that works directly in IFC. With the OpenAEC add-on you open and save models in your own environment, with versions and locking.

Later also as a cloud workstation in the browser.

  • BIM modelling in IFC
  • OpenAEC add-on for your files
  • Versions and locking
  • Later also in the browser
