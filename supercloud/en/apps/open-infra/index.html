Open Infra

OpenAEC · Topographic data for your work area, straight into your drawing.

Civil and infrastructure
Sign up with this appStart for free and switch this app on whenever you like.

About this app

An extension for Open CAD Studio that fetches the Dutch large-scale topography register (BGT) for your work area and sets it up as a drawing.

  • Select a work area
  • Fetch BGT layers
  • As a drawing in Open CAD Studio
  • Update with new data

How it runs
Desktop app you install locally. On the SuperCloud you sign in with your OpenAEC account and open and save files in your own environment, with versions and locking.
