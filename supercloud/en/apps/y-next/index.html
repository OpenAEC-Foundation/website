About this app

A simple workspace on top of ERPNext for daily work: mail, calendar, hours, projects and invoices on one screen. On the web, as a desktop app and on Android, also across several business environments.

  • Mail and calendar
  • Hours and projects
  • Invoices and quotes
  • Messages with colleagues
  • Web, desktop and Android
  • Several environments in one workspace
