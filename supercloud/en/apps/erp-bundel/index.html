About this app

The open-source ERP system at the heart of every business environment on the SuperCloud, together with a fixed set of additional open-source apps. You get it as one package: one account, one administration and one price per user.

  • Accounting, invoicing, purchasing and sales
  • Stock, projects, timesheets and reports
  • Staff, leave, expenses and payroll
  • Customer contact, opportunities and helpdesk
  • Knowledge base, training and team chat
  • Your own website and your own layout for quotes and invoices
