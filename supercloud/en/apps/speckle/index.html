Third-party open-source app, hosted or connected by OpenAEC. Its makers are not affiliated with OpenAEC.

About this app

Speckle connects design applications: share 3D models and data with your team and partners, view them in the browser and follow the changes. On the SuperCloud, Speckle runs in your own environment.

  • Share models between applications
  • 3D viewing in the browser
  • Versions and changes
  • Connectors for many design applications
