Pile Plan Studio

OpenAEC · Create a pile plan from loads and soil tests.

AvailableGeotechnicsBuildings
Sign up with this appStart for free and switch this app on whenever you like.

About this app

Draw up a pile plan based on the loads, the cone penetration tests and the geotechnical advice, including a cost indication.

  • Loads per axis
  • Link to soil tests
  • Pile positions and types
  • Cost indication
  • Drawing and report
