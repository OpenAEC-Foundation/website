Open BIM Validator Studio

OpenAEC · Check IFC models against IDS requirements.

AvailableBuildings
Sign up with this appSwitch this app on in your environment whenever it suits you.

About this app

Record your information requirements in IDS and check every delivered IFC model against them. For each element, you can see what is missing or incorrect.

  • Check against IDS
  • Overview of issues per element
  • Report for the delivering party
  • Also as a service for automatic checks
