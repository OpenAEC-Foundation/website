Open PDF Studio

AvailableAlso available separatelyGeneral

About this app

A complete PDF workspace for drawings and documents: annotate, measure to scale, stamp and verify signatures. Text in scanned documents becomes searchable.

  • Annotate and stamp
  • Measure to scale in drawings
  • Import DWG and DXF
  • Verify signatures
  • Text recognition in scans
