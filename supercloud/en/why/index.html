Why us

One environment for the whole construction company

Whether you work with five people or five hundred: the same open tools, in a way that fits the size of your organisation.

Why the OpenAEC SuperCloud

  • DATA

    Your data, your control

    Your data lives in your own environment, encrypted with your own key. You decide who has access, and you can always export everything.

  • { }

    Open source

    All software is open source: verifiable, no lock-in, and it runs on your own servers too.

  • DE

    Hosted in Europe

    On Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany. Our contract runs through its European entity, Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL in Luxembourg. Your data does not leave the EU.

  • AI

    AI across all your tools

    One AI assistant that can work with all your tools at once: read your model, have it calculated and record the result, through the same APIs and the same open language (IFCX).

  • AEC

    Built with practitioners

    Built by engineers, together with engineering firms and construction companies that use the tools every day.

  • IFCX

    IFCX as a generic format

    All your building data in one open, generic format that any program can read. Based on the open IFC standard, so never dependent on a single vendor.

  • LOCAL

    Full data copy at your office

    Alongside the cloud, a full copy of all your data is kept locally at your own office and updated automatically. So you can always carry on, even without internet.

50 employees and more

For large companies

  • Your own environment

    Your own servers and network with no other customers on them, or on-premises in your own server room.

  • Connect to what you have

    Every tool as an API too, with one key and in IFCX, so the SuperCloud fits into your existing systems.

  • Control and oversight

    Roles and permissions per environment, logs, support access only with permission, and AI you can restrict to the EU.

  • Agreements that fit

    Data processing agreement, certified infrastructure in Frankfurt and a dedicated contact.

  • Implementation and custom work

    OpenAEC Studio BV sets up the environment, helps with the switch and builds custom tools where needed.
