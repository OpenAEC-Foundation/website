OpenAEC SuperCloud Terms and Conditions

These terms apply to all use of the OpenAEC SuperCloud, both free and paid.

1. Who we are

The OpenAEC SuperCloud is a service of OpenAEC Studio BV, established in Dordrecht. Our details are shown at the bottom of this page. In these terms, OpenAEC Studio BV is referred to as 'we' and the organisation or person using the service as 'the customer'.

2. The service

The SuperCloud is an online environment with apps, file storage, ERP and AI functions, which the customer assembles itself from building blocks. The service also includes desktop apps that work together with the environment.

  • The software in the SuperCloud is open source. The software itself is governed by the licences of each individual project; these terms cover its hosting, management and support.
  • We may improve the service and replace components, provided that the functions the customer uses remain available. We will announce any material change in advance.

3. Registration and account

The customer registers at cloud.open-aec.eu and confirms its email address. This creates a free environment. A paid component takes effect as soon as the customer activates it.

  • The customer provides accurate information and keeps login credentials confidential. Each user has their own account.
  • The customer is responsible for what its users do in the environment.
  • Anyone registering on behalf of an organisation declares that they are authorised to do so.

4. Prices

Prices are listed on the pricing page and apply per month, excluding VAT. The price applicable at the time a component is activated is decisive.

  • For components charged per user, the number of users for whom the component is enabled is counted.
  • AI credit is charged based on usage.
  • We will announce any price change at least 30 days in advance. The customer may then cancel with effect from the date the change takes effect.

5. Payment

Payment is made monthly in advance via our payment service provider Stripe, by iDEAL or SEPA Direct Debit. Larger organisations may pay by invoice on request, with a payment term of 14 days.

  • If a payment fails, we will send a reminder. If payment has still not been received 14 days after that reminder, we may suspend paid components. The customer's data will be retained.
  • The customer must raise any objection to an amount within 30 days of the debit or invoice.

6. Term and cancellation

The agreement runs on a monthly basis and may be cancelled each month, without a notice period, with effect from the end of the current month. Cancellation can be done in the environment itself or by email. The customer may terminate a free environment at any time.

  • After termination, the customer can still export its data for 30 days. We then delete it, including backups, within the retention period applicable to those backups.
  • We may terminate the agreement with three months' notice, or with immediate effect in the event of serious misuse.

7. Service level and availability

Each environment has a service level: Basic (included), Business or Premium. The level determines the response times, the committed availability and the service credit if we fail to meet it.

  • The content of each level is described on the pricing page under Service levels. That description forms part of these terms.
  • We announce planned maintenance in advance and schedule it outside office hours as far as possible.
  • A service credit is set off against a subsequent month and is the sole compensation for failure to meet the availability.

8. Use of the service

The customer uses the service in accordance with the law and these terms.

  • Not permitted: storing or sharing unlawful content, attacking or overloading the service, circumventing security, or using the service for spam or malware.
  • In the event of misuse, we may restrict access to the component concerned. We will notify the customer immediately, stating the reason.

9. Data and privacy

The data in the environment is and remains the customer's. We use it solely to provide the service.

  • The environment runs in the EU, at Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt. The contract for this is with Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL.
  • For personal data in the environment, the customer is the controller and we are the processor. This is governed by the data processing agreement pursuant to Article 28 GDPR, which forms part of the agreement and which the customer receives signed on request.

AI functions are optional and can be switched on or off per environment. Processing takes place within the EU, via Amazon Bedrock in a European region.
