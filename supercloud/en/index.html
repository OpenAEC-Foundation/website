The problem

Construction firms work with separate programs that don't understand each other

35% of working time

Information gets lost

More than 14 hours a week go into searching for project information, resolving issues and fixing mistakes.

Source: research into data and communication in construction, FMI (US), 2018

Fragmented work

Each program with its own login, its own interface and its own file format. What is in one has to be entered again in the next.

Locked into closed formats

Proprietary file formats and licences make switching expensive. Whoever wants to move loses data or time.

How it works

From separate programs to one connected environment

  1. 01

    One account

    One login for all apps, with two-step verification or a passkey.

  2. 02

    Your own environment

    A private environment in an EU data centre, encrypted with your own key.

  3. 03

    Choose your apps

    Switch on apps from the marketplace. The standard ones are already there.

  4. 04

    One language: IFCX

    All apps read and write the same open model, so drawings, models and calculations work together without conversion.

On-premises

Prefer your own servers?

All apps are open source. If you want to run everything in your own server room or with your own hosting provider, you can. OpenAEC Studio BV helps with installation, management and updates.
