Privacy

Privacy statement

This statement covers this website of the OpenAEC SuperCloud.

Who is responsible

OpenAEC Studio BV is responsible for this website.

What data

This website does not ask you for anything. There is no form and we do not keep track of who visits the site. Like any web server, the server processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the page. We do not use it to follow you.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics services and loads nothing from other parties.

Signing up

If you sign up for the SuperCloud, you do so on cloud.open-aec.com. The privacy statement of the service itself explains which data we process there and why.

Your rights

You can view, correct or delete your data. You can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority.
