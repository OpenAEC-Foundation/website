Sign up for the SuperCloud

Signing up takes four steps. Send us your details and we will prepare the rest for you.

  1. Your details

    Name, country, address, email address and optionally your company.

  2. Confirm your email

    You receive a confirmation at the email address you provide.

  3. Choose licences

    Apps, storage, AI and the number of users, with the monthly amount.

  4. Adjust later

    Change everything in the admin environment whenever you like.
