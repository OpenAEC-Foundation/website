OpenAEC SuperCloud Data Processing Agreement

This data processing agreement forms part of the terms and conditions of the OpenAEC SuperCloud and applies to all personal data that we process on behalf of the customer.

Version 1.0, 22 October 2026
Draft version. This text will undergo legal review before the first paid agreement takes effect. The customer receives a signed copy on request.

1. Parties and roles

The customer is the controller and OpenAEC Studio BV is the processor within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This agreement remains in force for as long as we process personal data on behalf of the customer. In the event of a conflict, this agreement takes precedence over the terms and conditions insofar as the processing of personal data is concerned.

2. Subject matter, nature and purpose

We process personal data solely to provide the SuperCloud: hosting, storage, making data available, making backups, support and, if the customer enables them, performing AI functions.

  • Types of data: account and contact details of users, data in the customer's files, projects and ERP records, and technical and log data about use.
  • Data subjects: the customer's users, and persons about whom the customer records data in the environment, such as employees, clients, suppliers and contact persons.
  • The customer does not place special categories of personal data in the environment unless it has a legal basis for doing so and has agreed this with us in advance.

3. Processing on instructions

We process the personal data only on the documented instructions of the customer. The agreement, the settings the customer chooses in the environment and its requests to support count as instructions. If we consider an instruction to infringe the law, we will notify the customer immediately.

4. Confidentiality

Everyone who has access to the customer's personal data on our behalf is bound by confidentiality. Access by support takes place only with the customer's permission and is recorded in a log that the customer can inspect.

5. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures in accordance with Article 32 GDPR. The measures are described on the Security page and include:

  • storage and processing in the EU, at Amazon Web Services in the Frankfurt region, with encrypted backups in a second EU region;
  • a dedicated encryption key per customer, encryption at rest and in transit;
  • separate environments per customer, sign-in with two-factor authentication or a passkey, and roles and permissions per user;
  • logs of administrative actions and support access;
  • regular security updates and testing of backup restores.

6. Sub-processors

The customer authorises the engagement of the following sub-processors. With each sub-processor we have arrangements that offer at least the same protection as this agreement.

  • Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL: hosting, storage and the processing of AI requests via Amazon Bedrock, in the EU.
  • Stripe: payment processing, solely for the customer's payment and billing data.
  • We will notify a new or replacement sub-processor at least 30 days in advance. The customer may object on reasonable grounds within that period; if we cannot reach agreement, the customer may terminate the agreement with effect from the date of the change.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

We do not process personal data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). If the customer itself enables an AI model outside the EU, that transfer takes place on its instructions and only for the requests sent to that model.

8. Rights of data subjects

We provide the customer with reasonable assistance in responding to requests from data subjects, such as access, rectification, erasure and portability. Any request we receive is forwarded to the customer. The environment includes functions to export and delete data.

9. Data breaches

We notify the customer of a personal data breach without undue delay, and no later than 48 hours after we have become aware of it.

  • To the extent known, the notification describes what happened, which data and data subjects are affected, the likely consequences and the measures we are taking.
  • The customer decides whether to inform the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) and the data subjects. We assist with this.

10. Assistance and audits

We provide the customer with reasonable assistance with a data protection impact assessment and with questions from the supervisory authority. We give the customer the information needed to verify that we comply with this agreement.

  • An audit by or on behalf of the customer may take place once a year, with at least 30 days' notice, by an independent expert bound by confidentiality. The costs are borne by the customer, unless the audit reveals a material shortcoming on our part.

11. End of processing

After the agreement ends, the customer can still export its data for 30 days. We then delete the personal data, including from backups once their retention period expires, unless a legal obligation to retain it applies. On request, we confirm the deletion in writing.

12. Liability and governing law

Liability is governed by Article 12 of the terms and conditions. This agreement is governed by Dutch law. Disputes will be submitted to the competent court, the district court of Rotterdam, the Netherlands.
