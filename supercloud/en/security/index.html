Where your data lives

Your data stays in the EU

All environments run in a data centre in the European Union. Backups are kept in a second EU region.

  • AWS region Frankfurt (eu-central-1)
  • Every customer gets their own database and their own file storage
  • Encrypted backups in a second EU region

Separated from other customers

Every environment stands on its own: its own database, its own storage and its own key. Larger customers can choose their own servers and their own network, with no other customers on them.

A separate key per customer

Your data is encrypted with a key that only applies to your environment. When you leave, we destroy that key, which also makes all backups unreadable.

Encrypted in transit and at rest

Traffic always travels encrypted over https. Stored data, files and backups are encrypted as well.

Who can access it

Signing in

You sign in with one OpenAEC account for all apps, with two-step verification or a passkey. As an administrator you can make two-step verification mandatory for your whole environment.

  • Short sessions that expire automatically
  • Disabling an account takes effect in all apps at once

You decide who sees what

You set roles and permissions per environment. Users only see the projects, folders and data they have access to. Guests are free and only see what you share with them.

Sharing files safely

Send large files with a link instead of as an attachment.

  • An expiry date and a password per link
  • Revoke links at any time
  • Recipients do not need an account to download

Support only with your permission

OpenAEC staff cannot simply look inside your environment.

  • Support access only when you grant it, for 24 hours or 7 days
  • Every access is recorded in a log you can view yourself
  • Emergency access only during an outage or security incident, with a reason and immediate notice to you

AI

AI without giving away your data

AI features run only through language models in the EU by default. Your data is not used to train models. If you want to use a model outside the EU, you have to switch it on yourself, and it is clearly marked that your data leaves the EU.

  • Every model shows where it runs
  • As an administrator you can switch off models outside the EU for your whole environment
  • Limits per person and per environment, so usage never grows unnoticed

AI only sees what you may see

The AI search index belongs to your own environment and follows the same permissions as the apps. AI only finds documents the user is allowed to open, and conversations are stored in your own environment.

Operations and continuity

Operations without manual work

The entire infrastructure is defined in code. Every change is reviewed and recorded beforehand; nobody changes servers by hand.

Backups you can restore

Backups are made automatically and restored automatically as a test, so we know they work. An extra copy is kept in long-term storage.

Monitoring

The systems are monitored continuously. If something fails, our team is alerted immediately; support is available during office hours.

Email from your own domain

Email from your environment is sent from your own domain, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Recipients can see a message really comes from you, and emails that do not arrive show up in an overview.

Agreements

Data processing agreement

OpenAEC Studio BV processes your data on your behalf, as the GDPR calls it. Your subscription comes with a data processing agreement, including a list of the parties we use for it.

If something goes wrong

If a security incident affects your data, we report it without undue delay: what happened, which data is involved and what we are doing about it. That way you can meet your own reporting obligations.

Found a vulnerability?

Please let us know. We take every report seriously, tell you what we do with it and are happy to credit you if you wish.

Export and leaving

You can export everything at any time: files with their folder structure, data as CSV and a full database dump. Leaving happens in four steps.

  • Cancel at the end of the month
  • 30 days read-only, so you can export everything
  • 30 days frozen, in case you want to come back
  • Key destroyed, everything deleted, and a certificate of deletion

Open source

All software on the SuperCloud is open source. You can verify what happens to your data, and you can run the same apps on your own servers.

On-premises too

If you want to keep your data entirely in-house, the apps can also run on your own servers or with your own hosting provider. OpenAEC Studio BV helps with installation, management and updates.

Who is responsible for what

Security in the cloud is shared work. This is how it is divided on the SuperCloud.

AWS the infrastructure

  • Physical security of the data centres
  • Hardware, network and power
  • Availability of the underlying cloud services

OpenAEC Studio BV the platform

  • Separation between customers and a key per customer
  • Updates, backups, monitoring and incident handling
  • Sign-in, AI gateway and logged support access

You your own environment

  • Who has access, and with which permissions
  • Making two-step verification mandatory
  • What you share, with whom and for how long
