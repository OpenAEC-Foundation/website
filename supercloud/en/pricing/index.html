    How the price is built up

    • You pay per user per month, plus the building blocks you choose: apps, storage, ERP and AI.
    • Every environment includes hosting in Frankfurt, encryption with your own key and backups in a second EU region.
    • Apps carry a fixed contribution to ongoing development of € 0, € 5 or € 15 per user. That keeps the tools maintained and secure.
    • AI credit is billed by usage. We review prices every six months and publish how they are built up.
