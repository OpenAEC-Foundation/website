About us

A reliable foundation for your business software

The OpenAEC SuperCloud comes from an IT company rooted in the daily practice of an engineering firm. We develop the tools the construction industry needs every day and make them openly available to everyone.

How it started

  1. 2009

    3BM

    3BM is founded in Zwijndrecht and grows into a partnership of structural and building engineering firms.

  2. 2019

    3BM Labs

    Within 3BM, 3BM Labs is launched with an explicit goal: everything open source. The first open tools for construction are created there, such as building.py and GIS2BIM.

  3. Jan 2026

    Impertio Studio BV

    On 1 January 2026 Impertio Studio BV is founded in Dordrecht: an IT company that grew out of 3BM Labs.

  4. Jul 2026

    OpenAEC Studio BV

    On 1 July 2026 a major investor comes on board and the IT company continues as OpenAEC Studio BV. The company provides the services around the open tools: hosting, implementation, custom work and support.

  5. 2026

    OpenAEC Foundation (in formation)

    A foundation in formation will safeguard the open-source tools for the long term, independent of commercial interests.

  6. Now

    OpenAEC SuperCloud

    One environment with all the software a construction company needs, from office and ERP to BIM, calculations and email. Take everything or start with one tool.

Who does what

  • OpenAEC Foundation (in formation)

    Safeguards the open-source tools for the long term and runs the knowledge network.

  • OpenAEC Studio BV

    Provides the SuperCloud and the services around it: hosting, implementation, custom work and support.

  • The community

    Engineers, developers and firms who use and improve the tools and maintain their own repositories.

Built for the long term

Our shareholders include the Van Dorp family, owners since 1985 of a family business in building services that is run for the long term.

  • Open source: the code stays available, whatever happens
  • A foundation in formation safeguards the tools for the long term
  • Open formats and full export: you can always leave, with all your data
  • Shareholders who think in the long term
