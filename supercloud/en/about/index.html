About us

Engineers who build their own software

The OpenAEC SuperCloud did not come from a software company but from the daily practice of an engineering firm. We build the tools we need every day ourselves, and open them up to everyone.

How it started

  1. 2009

    3BM

    Maarten Vroegindeweij starts as an independent engineer. Together with other engineers, 3BM is founded in Zwijndrecht and grows into a partnership of structural and building engineering firms.

  2. 2019

    3BM Labs

    Within 3BM, 3BM Labs is born with a radical goal: everything open source. The first open tools for construction are created there, such as building.py and GIS2BIM.

  3. 2026

    OpenAEC Studio BV

    In January 2026 the company that grew out of 3BM Labs is founded in Dordrecht. It provides the services around the open tools: hosting, implementation, custom work and support.

  4. 2026

    OpenAEC Foundation (in formation)

    A foundation in formation will safeguard the open-source tools for the long term, independent of commercial interests.

  5. Now

    OpenAEC SuperCloud

    All tools together in one European cloud, with one account and one open language: IFCX.

Who does what

  • OpenAEC Foundation (in formation)

    Safeguards the open-source tools for the long term and runs the knowledge network.

  • OpenAEC Studio BV

    Provides the SuperCloud and the services around it: hosting, implementation, custom work and support.

  • The community

    Engineers, developers and firms who use and improve the tools and maintain their own repositories.
